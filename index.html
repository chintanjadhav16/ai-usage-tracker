Install in under a minute

  1. Unzip the download

    Double-click ai-usage-tracker.zip. You'll get a folder called ai-usage-tracker. Keep it somewhere you won't delete it.

  2. Open your extensions page

    Paste this into the address bar, then turn on Developer mode (top-right corner).

    chrome://extensions

  3. Click “Load unpacked”

    Choose the ai-usage-tracker folder you unzipped in step 1.

  4. Open a chat

    Go to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or DeepSeek. The bar appears at the bottom of the page. If the chat was already open, refresh it once.

Works on

  • Gemini

What it does with your chats

Nothing leaves your browser. The extension reads the conversation text on the page and counts tokens on your own computer. It sends no prompts and no usage data anywhere. Percentages are an estimate based on the text in the open conversation, not an official reading from the AI provider.
